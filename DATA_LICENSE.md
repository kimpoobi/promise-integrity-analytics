# Data attribution and reuse

Source: Olist, Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist.
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce

The publisher lists CC BY-NC-SA 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

Retain attribution, indicate modifications, and follow the source license when redistributing data or adaptations. This project does not relicense the underlying dataset. Data-derived reports and the dashboard retain the source attribution and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 notice. Do not assume that a public dataset is suitable for commercial use without reviewing its terms.

Raw CSVs, row-level exports, warehouse files, and model predictions are excluded from the source archive and ignored by Git. Aggregated reports still derive from the source data and must retain attribution.

Transformations: order-level item aggregation, earliest-review selection, post-delivery review restriction, delivery-calendar comparison, category translation, segment aggregation, statistical summaries, a delay model, and explicitly hypothetical re-scoring/cost scenarios.

No customer names, street addresses, free-text reviews, or secrets appear in the dashboard. Anonymous order IDs are retained only in local row-level analytical exports.
