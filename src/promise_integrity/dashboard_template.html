Decision discipline

Only handoff-time attributes enter the model. Delivery outcomes and reviews are excluded from features.

The model covers delivered orders with valid stage timestamps. It has not been prospectively tested and cannot estimate risk for cancelled or unresolved orders.

Change the definition. Watch the metric.

Global scenarios using observed deliveries. These controls do not represent actual ETA revisions, recovered revenue, or measured intervention effects.

Cost exposure under explicit assumptions

Proxy contribution = item value × assumed margin − assumed recovery cost. Not accounting profit. Freight charged is not carrier cost; actual refunds and recovery expenses are unknown.

Definitions you can audit

Observed delivery performance

Eligible: delivered status, valid purchase and delivery timestamps, a recorded estimate no earlier than purchase, and at least one item.

On time: actual delivery calendar date is on or before the recorded estimate date. Delivery later on the same date counts as on time.

Hidden failure proxy

Review score ≤ 2 among on-time orders with a first review answered at or after delivery. Duplicate reviews are reduced to the earliest response before this timing rule.

Missing or pre-delivery reviews are excluded from the rate denominator. They are never treated as satisfied customers.

Uncertainty and comparability

Segment intervals use Wilson 95% intervals. The global late/on-time review gap uses a 1,000-repeat purchase-week cluster bootstrap. Neither establishes causality.

Basket comparisons are standardized to common customer-state weights. A Simpson reversal is tested, not assumed.
